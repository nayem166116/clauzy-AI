Scope

What this page does and does not do

The public analyzer reads one clause in isolation. The workspace reads the whole instrument, which is a materially different exercise.

  • 01

    One clause, no context

    Definitions, carve-outs and cross-references living elsewhere in the agreement are invisible here. A liability cap that looks harsh may be fine once you read the exclusions in the next clause.

  • 02

    Market baseline, not your playbook

    Severity here reflects a general commercial standard. In the workspace, severity reflects the positions your own general counsel signed off, which is what actually determines whether a clause is acceptable.

  • 03

    Not legal advice

    Clauzy is a review tool used by qualified people, not a substitute for them. Nothing on this page creates a lawyer-client relationship, and no output should be sent to a counterparty without a human deciding it is right.
