A contract is not prose to be summarised. It is a numbered instrument where a single unqualified sentence can outlive the deal it was written for.

So Clauzy works the way a reviewer does: clause by clause, in order, against a written standard — and it shows its work every time it proposes an edit.

02 / Capability

What the review actually produces

Four outputs, delivered on every document. Nothing here needs a human to reformat it before it goes back to the other side.

  • 01

    Clause segmentation

    DOCX · PDF · scanned

    Clauzy splits the document into numbered clauses and sub-clauses, keeps cross-references intact, and reads defined terms as a graph rather than as loose capitalised words. Schedules and annexes are treated as part of the same instrument.

  • 02

    Playbook scoring

    Your positions

    Each clause is scored against your preferred position, your fallback, and your walk-away. A clause that is merely unusual is flagged differently from one that breaches a hard rule your general counsel set.

  • 03

    Drafted redlines

    Tracked changes

    Suggestions arrive as clause-level replacement text in your own drafting voice, exported as native tracked changes with a comment explaining the rationale and the rule reference behind it.

  • 04

    Negotiation memo

    One page

    A short internal note ranking what is blocking, what is worth trading, and what to concede first — so the person on the call knows the order of the argument before it starts.
